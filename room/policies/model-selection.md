# Model selection and effort

Own quality, time and cost together. Judge ambiguity, scope, verification difficulty, failure
cost and observed results before delegating. Model identity and effort are separate choices;
effort labels are not comparable token budgets across models or providers. A smaller model at
xhigh may cost more per successful outcome than a stronger model at medium.

Lead makes the final task-level choice for Peer/Lens within the Human's room allowlist. Supervisor and HQ
may intervene with evidence, adjust the framing or ask for escalation. Record the difficulty,
provider, exact model, effort and short rationale in the durable assignment. Peer and Lens may
recommend a change, and apply the same policy to their own delegation. Never bypass a room
restriction by choosing an ungoverned provider or a harness-local subagent.

Use routine for bounded edits with straightforward checks; standard for implementation with
known constraints; complex for uncertain diagnosis, cross-component changes and demanding
review; frontier for unusually ambiguous, high-risk or previously unsuccessful work. These
are judgments, not a mandatory escalation ladder. Start with the least costly setting likely
to meet the acceptance conditions; go directly to a stronger choice when the evidence warrants
it. Reassess after failed checks or a change in scope. Avoid repeating unsuccessful cheap runs.

Room priorities express the Human's preferences. The default worker policy uses Sol medium for
routine work, Sol high or Sonnet medium for standard implementation, and Sol high or Sonnet xhigh
for complex work. Opus/Astra are frontier routes, available through an explicit model selection or
frontier assessment. If no model or difficulty is supplied, selection uses the standard band; an
unavailable economical route is reported rather than silently falling through to a premium model.
A saved custom room allowlist remains authoritative and is never rewritten by an update.

HQ, Supervisor and Lead default to Sol/Sonnet, with the room's owner effort preference. They may
explicitly select any available model/effort. Use Astra/Opus for a named hard decision, uncertain
diagnosis or integration risk; explain why the economical setting is insufficient. Role seniority
alone is not a reason for a premium model. Do not inherit an expensive parent model into a worker.
Peer owns sustained implementation; expensive owners should make the decision and hand back the
bounded work. Small direct fixes and work where handoff costs more remain legitimate exceptions.

Consult the target role's live provider catalog and pass model and effort explicitly. Never
assume that an API feature is supported by an installed harness. If a requested setting is
unavailable, report it and choose another allowed setting deliberately. Compare representative
tasks by verified outcome, retries, latency and total token/cost use; revise preferences from
that evidence rather than model branding. Room settings apply to new launches. Preserve the
assignment and prior results when moving work to a fresh session after a policy change.
